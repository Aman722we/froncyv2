"I'm not looking / Not interested" 🔴 Disqualify "No problem! Thanks for letting me know." Move on immediately!

⚖️ Part 5: 10 Rules for Selling

1. Don't explain technical architecture / scrapers.
2. Don't start with "Do you check career pages?"
3. Don't claim LinkedIn/Naukri post late.
4. Don't promise guaranteed interviews.
5. Don't dump entire feature list in msg 1.
6. Don't discount before understanding objection.
7. Don't pitch people who clearly aren't looking.
8. Always show a real alert when demonstrating.
9. Make message relevant to their role & stack.
10. Never pretend someone is looking if not said so.
